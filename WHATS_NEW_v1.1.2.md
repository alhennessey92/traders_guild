# App Store "What's New" — v1.1.2

(Paste into App Store Connect → Version Information → "What's New in This Version".)

---

Grow your guild with brand-new invite tools 🎉

• Invite & refer friends — share your guild in a tap, straight to X, Discord, Messages and more. Send an invite link or QR code, and earn reputation when people you invite join.

• Your guild, one link — every guild now has its own shareable address. Tap it anywhere and it opens right to your guild in the app.

• Easier onboarding — new traders now start in a System Guild to explore and learn the ropes before joining a community.

• A fresher, more responsive feel — redesigned notifications and snappier, more reliable sharing throughout.

• Bug fixes and stability improvements, including a smoother sign-up.
