# Community Guidelines

**Last updated: 6 May 2026**

Traders Guild is a community for people who care about markets, trading, and learning from each other. These guidelines exist to keep the platform useful, respectful, and safe for everyone.

By using Traders Guild you agree to follow these rules. We may remove content or restrict accounts that break them.

## What we expect

**Be respectful.** Critique ideas, not people. Disagree without insulting, harassing, or attacking other members.

**Be honest.** Don't misrepresent your track record, credentials, performance, or affiliations. Don't impersonate other people or companies.

**Be helpful.** Share your reasoning, not just conclusions. Other members learn the most when you explain *why* you see a setup the way you do.

**Be appropriate.** Keep posts on-topic. This is a trading and markets community.

## What is not allowed

The following will be removed and may result in suspension or a permanent ban:

### Market manipulation and pump-and-dumps
- Coordinated promotion of a security to inflate its price.
- "Get in now before it moons" hype on illiquid tickers.
- Misleading claims about insider information.

### Financial advice and "guaranteed" returns
- You may share opinions and analysis, but you must not present them as personalised financial advice.
- No claims of guaranteed profits, risk-free strategies, or "100% win rate" systems.
- Signal-selling, paid groups, and "DM me for picks" content is not allowed.

### Scams and fraud
- Phishing, fake giveaways, fake support accounts.
- Cryptocurrency airdrop scams, recovery scams, romance scams.
- Pyramid schemes, MLMs, "trading academy" upsells with unverifiable results.

### Harassment and hate
- Personal attacks, threats, or stalking.
- Hate speech or slurs targeting people based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any protected characteristic.
- Doxxing — sharing someone's private information without consent.

### Spam and self-promotion
- Repeated posting of the same content.
- Promotion of unrelated products or services.
- Affiliate links without disclosure.
- Bot-driven engagement, vote manipulation, or fake accounts.

### Inappropriate content
- Sexually explicit material.
- Graphic violence or gore.
- Anything that puts minors at risk.

### Illegal activity
- Insider trading.
- Sharing or promoting access to pirated content, hacking tools, or stolen data.
- Anything that violates the law in your jurisdiction or ours.

## Reporting

If you see content or behaviour that breaks these guidelines, report it. Use the in-app report controls or email **support@tradersguild.co**. We review every report and act on those that breach the rules.

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity and history, we may:

1. Remove the offending content.
2. Issue a warning.
3. Temporarily restrict your account (e.g. mute, prevent posting).
4. Permanently suspend or ban the account.

Serious violations — manipulation, fraud, threats, content that endangers minors — result in immediate permanent bans without warning.

## Appeals

If you believe action was taken in error, email **support@tradersguild.co** with your username and a brief explanation. We will review and respond.

## Why these rules matter

Markets reward clarity of thought. A community full of noise, scams, and bad-faith actors is useless. We enforce these guidelines to keep Traders Guild a place where serious traders want to spend time.

Thanks for helping us build it.
