# What's New in Traders Guild 1.1.6

## Share markers anywhere

Share your guild-visible chart markers after placement. Send them directly to a
guild member, post your own markers to X or Discord, copy a link, or use the iOS
share sheet. Public marker links are created securely only when you choose to
share.

## Open shared markers in the app

Marker links now take you straight back to the relevant chart marker when
Traders Guild is installed, with clearer guidance when a marker is unavailable
or belongs to a guild you have not joined.

## Better guild invitations

Guild invitations have a more polished share experience, with improved previews
and easier sharing to Discord, X, Messages, and other apps.
